Our Story

From a Kakheti kitchen to your table

A Feast for Every Guest

In Georgia, a guest is a gift from God. Supra Tbilisi was opened in 2015 by the Beridze family to share that hospitality with everyone who walks through our door.

Our bread is baked in a traditional clay tone oven, our herbs come from small farms near Telavi, and our wine is made in qvevri buried in the earth, a method listed by UNESCO.

— The Beridze Family

Our Journey

  1. 2015 Opened with 6 tables and one tone oven.
  2. 2018 Added the wine cellar with 40 qvevri bottles.
  3. 2021 Introduced the Friday live folk music nights.
  4. 2025 Voted Best Georgian Restaurant by local diners.
